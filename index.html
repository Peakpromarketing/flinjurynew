Actionable Checklist

What To Do After an Accident in Pinellas County

After an accident in Palm Harbor, Clearwater, Largo, or surrounding areas, taking the right steps early can protect both your health and your potential injury claim. Seeking prompt medical attention helps identify hidden injuries and creates important documentation, while gathering photos, witness information, and scene details can preserve key evidence.

  1. 1 Seek medical care immediately, even if injuries are not obvious, to ensure proper diagnosis and official documentation.
  2. 2 If safe, take photos of the scene, vehicles, visible injuries, and any hazards involved.
  3. 3 Collect names and contact information of all eyewitnesses.
  4. 4 Avoid admitting fault or discussing the accident on social media platforms.
  5. 5 Be cautious about giving recorded statements to insurance adjusters before understanding your legal rights.
  6. 6 Contact a personal injury attorney early so evidence can be preserved and your claim properly evaluated.

Legal guidance can help manage insurance communications and prevent statements that may harm your case.
