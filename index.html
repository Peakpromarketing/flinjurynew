Pinellas County & Tampa Bay Personal Injury Lawyers

Offices in Palm Harbor, Clearwater, Holiday, and Largo

At Andreopoulos Law, we are a premier personal injury law firm representing accident victims across Pinellas County, Tampa Bay, and Pasco County. Founded in 2002, our legal practice provides aggressive representation for individuals and families in Clearwater, Tarpon Springs, Largo, Holiday, and Palm Harbor. Each claim is managed directly by veteran trial attorney John Andreopoulos, offering over 23 years of dedicated legal experience and a proven record of 88+ jury trials tried to verdict.

Our firm specializes in high-stakes personal injury litigation, including car accidents, commercial truck crashes, motorcycle collisions, slip and falls, negligent security, and wrongful death claims. We regularly handle complex motor vehicle accident cases originating along Tampa Bay's most hazardous transit corridors, including U.S. Highway 19, McMullen Booth Road, Tampa Road, Gulf-to-Bay Boulevard, and East Bay Drive.

Insurance companies regularly analyze whether a law firm actively tries cases in court. By preparing every client’s case from day one as if it will be decided by a Florida jury, we leverage maximum pressure during settlement negotiations to pursue full compensation for medical bills, lost wages, and long-term rehabilitation.
